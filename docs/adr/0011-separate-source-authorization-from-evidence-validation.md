# Separate source authorization from evidence validation

Supersedes the `untrusted.json` marker decision in [ADR-0006](0006-prove-evidence-identity-from-consumer-visible-records.md) while retaining its evidence-reconciliation requirements.

The scheduling-stop policy below is superseded by
[ADR-0014](0014-isolate-acquisition-failures-by-suite.md); the other decisions remain in force.

CUBRID's configured company CI is an authorized evidence source, but its downloaded records remain unvalidated until commit, execution, shard provenance, counts, and verdicts reconcile. The collector retains bounded partial raw evidence through append-only command-wide collection observations, while an immutable suite `summary.json` is the publication seam that makes that suite consumable as validated evidence. Response bodies are streamed through temporary files to raw storage under a configurable collection-wide fetch ceiling; each complete body is promoted immediately and can be reused after digest and length verification even when a later stage of its shard fails. Incomplete temporary bodies are removed and their consumed bytes and failure are recorded in the observation. An existing suite summary is reused without network acquisition only after its identity, schema, and referenced raw-file digests validate; contradictory immutable evidence fails and requires explicit cleanup.

Fetch concurrency follows command-line, environment, configuration-file, then built-in precedence, and both its effective value and source are recorded. The operational default is selected by staged `10/20/40/80` measurement against one representative completed all-suite execution rather than CPU count or domain semantics. Benchmark trials use isolated temporary evidence roots that are removed after a retained report records source identity, settings, wall times, peak memory, and failures. A failed acquisition stops scheduling new shards, drains already-running work, and records deterministic retained, failed, and not-attempted shard states without rewriting provider evidence.
